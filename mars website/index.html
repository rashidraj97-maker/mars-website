FAQ

Questions we often get.

Can't find your answer? Ask us on WhatsApp.

Why is MARS different from others?

Our Training method, dedicated certified trainers and post-Training support. We assess your needs before Training and use case studies from real work.

Do you train online or in person?

Both. Online Training reaches teams in any city without travel, which saves time and money. Classroom and on-site sessions are available across India through our partner venues.

How do I enroll in a course?

Choose a course and send an enquiry, or enroll and pay online. Our team then confirms your batch, date and time slot.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes. Every course ends with an exam, and after passing it you receive a course completion certificate from MARS Training & Solution.

Do you help with job placement?

Yes. We assist our students with job placement, including interview preparation and guidance on finding the right role.

Do you conduct Training outside India?

Yes. Along with online and classroom Training across India, we also conduct overseas Training for organisations.

Can you train our whole department?

Yes. Corporate programmes are planned around a skills analysis of your team, at flexible hours, on-site or online.

Ready to upskill yourself or your team?
